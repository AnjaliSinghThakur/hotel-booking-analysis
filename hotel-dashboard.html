Key takeaways

  • Bookings made 180+ days ahead cancel 39.7% of the time, versus 8.4% within a week.
  • Online travel agency bookings cancel 35.4%, direct bookings 14.7%.
  • City Hotel cancels more (30.1%) than Resort Hotel (23.5%).
Source: Hotel Booking Demand dataset (Antonio, Almeida and Nunes, 2019). The "Undefined" market segment is left out because it has too few bookings, and non-refundable deposits (94.7% cancelled) are left out as a likely data quirk.
